# RT56 - Anozin's USA Bookmark Merge Wiki

Documentation for **RT56 - Anozin's USA Bookmark Merge**, a local-only patch for the Local Modpack. It is never published to the Workshop, because it carries another author's file.

## What the mod is

Anozin's USA Reimagined ships its own `common/bookmarks/the_gathering_storm.txt`, which takes the place of Road to 56's copy and brings back vanilla's 1936 country selection. This mod ships one merged copy of that file: Road to 56's bookmark with the ideas and focuses of the United States entry taken from Anozin's.

## Required mods and load order

- RT56 - VNR - UTT Standalone v14
- Anozin's USA Reimagined

The mod lists both as dependencies and loads after them. A new version of either mod means a new merge.
